# SPONSORS.md: what each tool does in Village (D15) and why it ranks for its track

Village: software that builds itself around your people. **Build → Run → Rebuild.** Every sponsor owns one stage of that loop; none is decorative. Use this file for the Devpost "Built with" section, the README sponsor table, and the 30-second pitch at each sponsor table.

| Sponsor | Its job in the loop | Where judges see it | Why it fits their track (their own words) | Odds (reviews) |

## 30-second sponsor-table pitches
- **Daytona:** "Village writes software for one family and changes it when their life changes. Every version is built and tested in a Daytona sandbox before it reaches Grandma. Here's a build we caught failing and fixed."
- **Arize:** "Our agent reads its own Arize evals. Session evals on Mom's calls flagged a scam pattern, and that's what made it rebuild her app: detect, investigate, fix, verify, then a human approves."
- **SurrealDB:** "Every fact in the family's memory knows who said it and when. Changes supersede instead of overwriting, three agents share it, and LIVE queries push it to every phone."
- **ElevenLabs:** "For Carmen the whole product is a phone call, in Spanish, with safety lines spoken word for word. And it calls her son to verify."
- **Anchor:** "No generated screen reaches Carmen until an Anchor browser has used it like she would. Watch the live view."
- **AWS:** "Claude on Bedrock does all the reasoning; production would publish each family's app to S3 + CloudFront."

**On stage, name three** (Daytona, Arize, SurrealDB) as the rebuild loop; the rest go on Devpost and at the tables.
