Village

Software that builds itself around your people.

One conversation about a group. A different app for each person in it. An agent that runs the group, changes the software when life changes, and refuses itself when it should.

The problem is one person

Carmen is 78, speaks Spanish, and will not learn an app.

  • A blood thinner twice a day, from a weekly organizer. Some nights she is not sure she took it.
  • Three children, three different lives: Ana coordinates, Marco forgets his night, Lucía is far away.
  • Last week a stranger called her as Marco and asked for $1,500.

So her software is three big buttons and a phone line. In Spanish. Read aloud.

Build

One conversation. Four pieces of software.

Ana talks for ten minutes. Village draws each person's app from the parts of the family's life their job touches. Marco's app is one question. Lucía's is what she can do from New Jersey.

Tabs come from the group's life areas. Every element comes from a fixed, validated catalog.

Run

Village is a participant, not a form.

  • It sits in the family chat. "Someone needs to take Mom to the doctor Friday" becomes a Friday plan item and a question to the people who can actually drive.
  • Every decision is labelled: did it on its own · asked a person · refused itself.
  • It remembers with history: lisinopril is 10 mg since Oct 3, and "as of Oct 2" still answers 5 mg.

Rebuild

Life changed. The software changed.

After the second money call in Marco's name, Village weighs three fixes and proposes one: answer unknown callers on Carmen's line first. It says why. It waits for Ana's yes.

Then it builds the new version in a sandbox, tests it, clicks through it at phone size, scores it, and patches every phone.

12 seconds from Ana's tap to every phone, live at 13:00 today.

Safeguards

The agent acts on its own. Code draws the lines it cannot cross.

specDaytona sandbox buildfloor tests, generated per villageAnchor phone-size checkArize evalsblue / green release
  • The gate is pure Python, tested with the model off. The voice line speaks the gate's sentences word for word.
  • Money asked for in a family name is checked with that person before anything is said. The Oct 9 scam call never got past it.
  • Generated UI is catalog-only, validated on the server and the client. Elder text at least 28 px, contrast at least 7:1, a 911 contact on every elder app.
  • Sandboxes cannot reach the internet. The one build that failed today was caught at contrast 4.1:1, fixed by the Builder, and rebuilt before anyone saw it.

Any group

Same code. A block in a blackout.

Ana also lives on West 147th. When the block lost power in a heat wave, the same pipeline built a village for the neighborhood. Nothing from the family carried over except the code.

  • A coordinator, volunteers, a resident on oxygen, a parent with an infant, a deli with ice.
  • Village checks rumors against official sources before anything is broadcast, and calls the cooling centers itself to log who has power.
