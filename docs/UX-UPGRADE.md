# UX upgrade: four different apps, one chat, one room that shows the agent working (13:00, spec of record for the kit lanes)

**What the platform is, in one line the UI must prove:** Village looks at a group of people, works out each person's *job* in the group, and builds each of them the smallest piece of software that does that job; then it runs the group's week and changes itself when life changes, always asking a human before anything that matters.

So the four apps are not four orderings of the same cards. Each is shaped like a job. The group chat is where the village talks and where Village, the participant, reports what it did and asks for a yes. The Control Room is where you watch it think. Plain words everywhere; the complexity shows as *what it did and why*, never as ids.

Rules that make them different and helpful: each screen opens with the one line that person needs; content that is not their job is absent, not collapsed; counts come from events and are sourced; every autonomous action is visible as a row in words with the role name (Founder / Watcher / Verifier / Builder / Explainer); no rule ids, trace ids or member ids on phones.

## The group chat (D16), visible and alive
Where: a **Chat tab** on every family app (never on Carmen's; her chat is the phone line), plus a mirror on the Control Room. Village is a participant with its own bubble style (sage, the Village mark, third person: "Village stopped a scam call…"). Message kinds, each rendered differently: member (name, time), **Village** (what it did, with a source chip: "from Mamá's call, 12:23"), **relayed from a call** (amber, Spanish quote + English line), **proposal** (title + one sentence + Approve · Not now inline for leaders), **receipt** ("Every phone updated · rev 6 · built, tested, tried on a phone, scored"), **nudge** ("Marco, you're on tonight"), **answer** (to "@Village why…", with citation chips in words: "her words on the welcome call", "the rule: no money without the real person"). Composer with an "@Village" chip; "@Village why…" answers with sources. Seed the Reyes chat so the demo opens with a week of life in it (D16 fixture, now without lid wording).

## The Control Room: watch it think, never a log viewer
Header: Village · village pills · + New village · tagline. **Arc strip** under it: Build → Run → Rebuild → Anyone, the current beat lit. **Autonomy headline**: "Tonight Village did 3 things on its own · asked a person 1 · refused itself 1" (from rounds), each a tappable row. Founding scene (D33, done) → during Run: Live panel + captions + Marco's phone mirrored → Rebuild: Options (three fixes, two struck with their one-line reason, the winner "testing") → ReleaseStepper in words with real durations → the phones row flashing NEW → Anyone: the pill switch. Right column always: "What Village knows" (fact history, as-of) and the decisions log in words with role chips and mode chips; ids and traces behind a "details" toggle for the sponsor judges.

## How the complexity shows (plainly)
- Every agent action is a sentence with an actor and a result, never a JSON. "Watcher · weighed 3 fixes · chose: Village answers unknown callers" · "Builder · built rev 6 in a sandbox · 16 tests passed · caught a contrast failure and fixed it" · "Verifier · asked Marco · he said no in 12 s".
- Every rule is a sentence: "No money without the real person's yes" · "After 9:30 PM, no calls (her words)".
- Every number has a source chip. Every change has a "why" one tap away. Nothing on a phone that a 78-year-old's daughter would not say out loud.
